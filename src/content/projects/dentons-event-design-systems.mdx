## Dentons Brand Guidelines

Dentons’ visual identity uses organic and nature-based imagery as its standard approach. When additional context is needed, the hybrid style pairs an organic image with a non-organic one, such as architecture or infrastructure.

Both approaches use Dentons’ signature guidepost shape and overlay treatment, with one image in color and the other in black and white.

## Challenge

Each system needed to balance three constraints: remain unmistakably Dentons, create a distinct narrative for each event, and use imagery that felt relevant to its geographic and cultural context.

## 01 — Out & About 2024

Dentons’ annual internal business development campaign challenged teams across Australia to strengthen client engagement over a month. With connection as the central theme, I explored visual directions that brought together Australian landmarks and local natural elements.

### Vibrant Connections

This hybrid direction pairs Melbourne’s Arts Centre spire with Western Australia’s Hutt Lagoon. The spire’s intersecting structure became a metaphor for collaboration and connection, while the lagoon introduced a distinctly Australian organic element.

<CaseImage src={vibrant} alt="Arts Centre Melbourne spire paired with Hutt Lagoon, applied to an Out & About 2024 presentation on a laptop and a printed cover" />

### Bridging Sectors

I chose Melbourne’s Webb Bridge as a direct metaphor for connection and paired it with the Mottlecah, a flower native to Western Australia. The Dentons Orange-to-Gold gradient echoes the flower’s warm tones while contrasting with the blue sky.

<CaseImage src={bridging} alt="Webb Bridge paired with the Mottlecah flower, applied to an Out & About 2024 cover and banner in an orange-to-gold gradient" />

## 02 — Webinar on Book Banning

For a U.S. webinar on book-banning legislation, I avoided alarmist or politically charged imagery, using an owl and books to keep the focus on knowledge. Dentons Light Purple ties it back to the brand.

<CaseImage src={webinar} alt="Books and an owl paired for the Book Banning Webinar, shown as a LinkedIn post on a phone and a presentation on a laptop" />

## 03 — LAC Leadership Committee — London 2026

The event identity needed to translate two business priorities into a visual system: client-centric growth and global integration under Dentons’ “One Firm” model. With London as this year’s host city, I explored these ideas through recognizable local architecture.

### Integration and Coordination

Ivy represents organic growth through connection, while Tower Bridge, a London symbol of linking people and places, adds the idea of bringing regions and markets together. Combined, they form a metaphor for integration.

<CaseImage src={integration} alt="Tower Bridge paired with ivy, applied to a roll-up banner and a LAC Leadership Committee In Person Meeting slide in purple" />

### One Firm: Together in Motion

The River Thames symbolizes movement and connection, while the London Eye adds a sense of shared direction. Dentons Dark Blue echoes the river, tying the concept to the brand palette.
