## DiDi: The Orange Passenger

“The Orange Passenger” began as an urgent brief for Ecuador, inviting drivers to win cash prizes by picking up a mysterious passenger dressed in DiDi’s signature orange.

With a two-day turnaround and no photography to work with, I built the key visual through illustration. The campaign later expanded to other Latin American markets, including Chile, Colombia, and Costa Rica.
