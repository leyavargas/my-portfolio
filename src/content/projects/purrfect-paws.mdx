## Research

Secondary research examined the gap between cat and dog care, highlighting how misunderstandings about cats’ behavior and needs can create barriers to appropriate care.

Three themes informed the product concept:

- **Subtle signs of illness.** Cats often hide pain, making behavioral changes easy to overlook.
- **Stress around vet visits.** Preparing for appointments and finding cat-friendly care can be challenging.
- **The “low-maintenance” myth.** Perceptions of independence can lead owners to underestimate cats’ need for play, routine, and preventive care.

## Product Concept and UI

Purrfect Paws brings together educational guidance, care management, and connected tools to support everyday cat care, with features informed by research.

The home screen provides general care guidance, from information on household hazards and common cat myths to help finding specialized feline veterinarians.

<CaseImage src={homeScreens} alt="Home screen with care topics, plus the What not to do and Improve your relationship guides" />

Each cat’s profile provides a dedicated space to organize their individual care, combining practical features such as health records with sentimental touches like a paw-print keepsake.

<CaseImage src={profileScreens} alt="Profiles screen with two cats, Milo’s profile with options to add health records, a paw print, a feeder, a GPS finder, and a vet appointment, and the vet appointment calendar" />

## Visual Direction

The visual identity balances softness with a bold, quirky, and slightly irreverent personality, capturing the playful nature of cats and the unique bond they share with their owners. This approach shaped the creative direction across the logo, color palette, typography, and photography.

## Next Steps

Further development would focus on testing whether owners can find and use features without assistance, and whether their placement on the home screen or within individual cat profiles matches users’ expectations. These findings would guide adjustments to the app’s structure and navigation before expanding the concept.
