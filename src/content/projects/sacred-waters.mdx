## Creative Direction

The creative direction began with the experience of drinking tea, a small moment of calm that lifts the day, like an oasis. This led to the idea of tea as something precious, and ultimately to the name **Sacred Waters**. That idea set the creative direction in the desert, where water is scarce.

### Logo: The Fennec Fox

Rather than relying on familiar tea symbols, I chose the fennec fox as the brand emblem: a desert animal that thrives with very little water.

## Premium, Yet Accessible

The goal was to create a brand that felt premium yet accessible: special enough to feel like a treat, but still part of everyday life. That balance shaped both the design and the packaging decisions.

Other formats, such as tins or round boxes, felt too exclusive for the brand, so I chose paper boxes with individual sachets. The sachets also make the tea easy to carry and enjoy anywhere, even outdoors. It's also why the brand uses tea bags rather than loose-leaf tea.

## Packaging

Each of the six varieties has its own color, inspired by its flavor or key ingredient. The challenge was to bring these colors together into a cohesive palette while staying true to the creative direction.

The collection is divided into two product lines that share the same packaging structure: blended teas feature a dark base, while herbal infusions use a light one, making them easy to tell apart at a glance.
