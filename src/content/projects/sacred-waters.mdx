## Creative Direction

The creative direction began with the experience of drinking tea: a small moment of calm where everything feels right. This led to the idea of tea as something precious, even sacred, and ultimately to the name **Sacred Waters**. The idea of a daily oasis grew from there, in the desert, where water is truly precious.

The goal was to create a brand that felt premium yet accessible: special enough to feel like a treat, but still part of everyday life. That balance shaped both the design and the packaging decisions.

### Logo: The Fennec Fox

Rather than relying on familiar tea symbols, I chose the fennec fox as the brand emblem: a desert animal adapted to life where water is scarce.

## Packaging

Each of the six varieties has its own color, inspired by its flavor or key ingredient. The challenge was to bring these colors together into a cohesive palette while staying true to the creative direction.

The collection is divided into two product lines that share the same packaging structure: blended teas feature a dark base, while herbal infusions use a light one, making them easy to tell apart at a glance.

Other formats felt too exclusive for the brand, so I chose paper boxes with individual sachets. The sachets also make the tea easy to carry and enjoy anywhere, even outdoors.
