## Creative Direction

The creative direction began with the experience of drinking tea: a small moment of calm where everything feels right. This led to the idea of tea as something precious, even sacred, and ultimately to the name **Sacred Waters**.

The idea of a daily oasis grew from there, set in the desert, where water is truly precious.

### Logo: The Fennec Fox

Rather than relying on familiar tea symbols, I chose the fennec fox as the brand emblem: a desert animal adapted to life where water is scarce.

### Typography & Color

Fragment gives the identity its character, while Object Sans keeps functional information clear. The palette draws from the colors of the desert landscape.

<CaseImage src={typeColor} alt="Color palette with Herbal green, Sand, Cactus, White, Sunrise blue, Flower pink, and Sunny orange, next to the Fragment Glare and Object Sans typefaces" />

## Packaging

Each of the six varieties has its own color, tied to its flavor or ingredient. The challenge was making these colors work well together while staying true to the creative direction. Cardboard boxes with individual sachets make the tea easy to carry and enjoy anywhere.
